# 拼贴插画与照片处理提示词

从本目录截图整理出的 10 种照片处理方式。上传原图后，按风格复制对应提示词即可。

`【照片主体】` 可改成具体对象，例如猫咪、海景、人物、花束。没写就默认处理整张照片。

| 编号 | 风格 | 结果 | 比例 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 真实照片 + 日系手帐 | 上半原图，下半水彩手帐拼贴 | 3:4 |
| 02 | 极简杂志剪贴 | 整张变成韩系剪贴插画 | 3:4 |
| 03 | Zine 电子明信片 | 上半原图，下半手绘主元素 + 色卡，可出正反面 | 2:3 |
| 04 | 旅行故事书丙烯手绘 | 上半原图，下半小主体大留白丙烯画 | 3:4 |

## 01 真实照片 + 日系手帐

适合风景、猫咪、小狗、人物、花束、挂件、蔬果等随手拍。上半保留拍摄感，下半变成低饱和手帐内页。

同一张图可以多出几次。纸张质感、低饱和、手绘线条这三件事写清楚，结果会比较稳。

```text
请把这张【照片主体】照片，做成 上半部分真实照片 + 下半部分手帐插画 的3:4竖版版式。
上半部分保留真实拍摄感、自然光影和生活氛围；下半部分将同一【照片主体】转化为 日系治愈手帐插画，风格为 简约线稿、水彩淡彩、纸张纹理、低饱和、拼贴排版。
加入 手写感标题、英文短句、日期、小印章、便签纸、胶带 等元素，整体 温柔、安静、清新、精致、有记录生活的感觉，适合做分享图，4K高清。
```

## 02 极简杂志剪贴

把照片重画成粉彩纸片拼贴，不是保留原图像素。韩系编辑感、撕纸边缘、大留白。人物会变成极简五官的纸雕剪影。

```text
将上传的图片转换为极简杂志剪贴拼贴风插画。
保留上传图片中的主体、姿势和整体概念，但将其重新诠释为一种温暖的手工编辑拼贴风格。

风格：极简杂志剪贴拼贴插画，扁平化分层纸片形状，柔和的粉彩纸张纹理，撕纸边缘，纸张投影效果，干净的黑色涂鸦点缀，手工剪贴本氛围，现代韩系编辑设计感，简单可爱的构图，大面积干净的白色留白。

角色：可爱的简化韩系人物，极简五官，轻松的小微笑，柔和圆润的比例，简单休闲的服装，纸雕分层剪影感。

构图：竖版 3:4 比例，主体略微靠下并偏离中心摆放，另一侧保留大面积空白，稀疏、轻盈、有呼吸感的布局，不要拥挤。

物件：只添加少量相关的拼贴道具，纸质便签，小爱心，植物，咖啡杯，窗户，胶带片，简单涂鸦图标。

文字排版：添加一个优雅的手写英文标题，与画面场景相匹配。
可使用以下简短短语：“Take a small break” “Soft little moment” “Everyday joy” “Quiet mood” “Good day, good mood”

氛围：平静、舒适、温暖、可爱、编辑感。

避免：真实照片质感，动漫风格，水彩，3D 黏土风，过于复杂的背景，拥挤的拼贴，奢华海报感，深色调，强烈硬阴影，杂乱文字。
```

## 03 Zine 电子明信片

截图里的风铃图就是这种：暖白纸、上方嵌原图、下方一个手绘主元素、左侧极简文字、三个取自原图的色块。需要正反面时，背面做成可写信的明信片。

原图始终是主体。不要拉伸、不要重绘上方照片、不要堆一排水彩小样。只选一个最能代表原图的元素来画。

### 正面

```text
Create a minimal portrait postcard front using the fixed V3 Lite structure. Ratio 2:3, warm ivory paper with subtle grain.

Use the actual source photo in the upper area. Preserve its exact original aspect ratio. Do not repaint, replace, stretch, or crop it unless cropping was explicitly requested. Center it horizontally, keep generous margins, add a very thin frame, and leave a small even paper gap between the photo and frame.

Keep a large blank transition area below the photo.

In the lower area, use:
- one compact metadata block on the lower left
- one large source-specific main motif on the lower right
- optionally one much smaller supporting motif near it
- exactly three small color swatches sampled from the source photo

Select the main motif based on visual attractiveness and source identity, not ease of extraction. Prefer the most distinctive color-rich and elegant feature in the image. When the source contains a dominant color feature, choose that feature as the main motif. Do not choose a dull neutral fragment when a stronger colored motif exists.

Render the main motif as a restrained hand-drawn editorial illustration whenever suitable. Preserve its original silhouette, major internal structure, identity, and source color character. Use controlled watercolor, gouache, ink, pencil, or cut-paper illustration treatment. Keep it clean, sharp, and clearly derived from the source photo.

Use a source crop only when exact fidelity is essential, such as faces, hands, text, or complex machinery.

The main motif should occupy roughly 28% to 38% of the card width and remain subordinate to the photo.

Use exactly three tiny source-derived color swatches: dominant color, dark structural color, and pale neutral or accent color.

Add only:
- title
- optional short subtitle
- LOCATION
- DATE
- small index number

Leave location and date values blank if not provided.

Do not add keyword lists, sample boxes, rows of cutouts, image cards, generic circles, large color blocks, wave doodles, badges, seals, logos, or long text.

Overall mood: minimal, airy, refined, source-specific, lightly hand-crafted, and collectible.
```

### 背面

```text
Create a matching unified postcard back using the same portrait 2:3 ratio, paper tone, thin-line style, and restrained visual language as the front.

Keep it functional and mostly blank.

Include:
- thin outer border
- one vertical divider slightly right of center
- stamp box in the upper-right
- 3 or 4 address lines on the right
- large blank message area on the left

Optionally add small POST CARD text near the upper-left and tiny metadata or index near the lower-left.

Do not add a large collage, palette, sample blocks, or decoration that reduces writing space.
```

## 04 旅行故事书丙烯手绘

截图里圣托里尼台阶那张就是这种。不是把照片描成线稿，而是把人物动作、风景轮廓、颜色、空间关系提炼成一个很小的手绘场景。

主体越小、留白越大、颜色越少，越容易出安静的高级感。同一张照片建议多刷几次。

```text
你是一名擅长极简插画、纸张媒介、丙烯色块绘画与视觉提炼的封面插画师。
请将我上传的单张照片制作成一张3:4竖版双分区艺术海报，画面上下严格按照 1:1 / 50% : 50% 分区。

上半部分：完整保留原始照片的主体、人物、建筑、动物、自然景观、空间关系与核心构图，保持真实摄影质感、自然光影和原有色彩氛围，仅允许轻微高级调色，不改变照片内容。

下半部分：根据上半部分照片重新进行视觉提炼与再创作。
提取照片中最具识别性的主体、轮廓、姿态、动作、空间关系与叙事关系，删除复杂细节，用纤细、轻微不稳定的手绘线条 + 少量明确的丙烯平涂色块重新表达。
不要写实复刻原图，也不要直接将照片转换成线稿。

插画主体必须小而集中，仅占下半部分约 10%-20%，四周保留大量留白，让画面呈现“小主体、大留白”的视觉关系。
背景使用粗糙白纸、米白纸或浅色艺术纸纹理，保留明显纸张颗粒感，仅用少量线条、笔触或简单色面暗示山脉、海面、草地、建筑、天空等环境。
颜色从原照片中提取，并压缩为不超过 4 种主要颜色。
色块采用完整、鲜明、克制的丙烯平涂效果，保留纸张颗粒、涂抹痕迹、干刷质感与轻微不规则边缘。线条负责提示结构，色块负责建立视觉主体。

可在插画下方加入少量简洁文字，例如：
英文标题 / 地点 / 年份 / 日期 / 编号 / 一句简短主题。
文字保持小尺寸、疏朗排版，与留白自然融合，不抢夺主体。

整体呈现艺术书封、独立出版物、旅行手账、儿童绘本式视觉设计感，画面安静、童趣、轻松、诗意、克制、高级，避免模板化设计。
```

## 05 现实 vs 记忆速写

上半原图，下半像只看了一秒再凭记忆画下来的连续线涂鸦。不要装饰、不要标题、不要把照片再画一遍。

```text
Transform the uploaded photo into a visual memory translation poster.

The final output must contain TWO sections.

Top section:
Display the original uploaded photograph.

Bottom section:
Display an ultra-minimal visual memory sketch extracted from the photo.

The final image should look like a comparison between reality and memory.

Layout Rules:
Vertical composition.
Top 50%: Original photo.
Bottom 50%: White background.
Place the visual memory sketch in the center of the white area.
Large margins. Generous negative space.
No borders. No frames. No decorative elements.
No captions. No explanations. No arrows. No labels.

Visual Memory Sketch Rules:
Do NOT recreate the photo realistically.
Identify only the most recognizable visual elements.
Reduce the scene to its visual essence.
Draw only what is necessary for recognition.

Style:
extremely simplified
visual essence only
memory captured in 5 seconds
naive observational drawing
continuous line doodles
poetic abstraction

Reduction Principle:
Reduce every object to the minimum number of strokes required for human recognition.
Recognition is more important than accuracy.

Important:
Create a visual memory.
'I saw this scene for one second, looked away, and then drew only what remained in my memory.'
```

## 06 蜡笔全身插画

截图里吃面那张就是这种。照片只提供身份、姿态、穿搭轮廓，最终必须重画成儿童画比例的蜡笔扁平肖像。原帖需要再传 2 张风格参考图。

```text
【任务】基于用户上传真实照片做 image-to-image 风格转换，输出 1:1 方图。照片只作为人物身份、姿态、穿搭轮廓参考，final 画面必须重新绘制成极简扁平手绘插画。

【参考】附件作为 visual style reference，只参考：纯白背景、稚拙手绘线条、蜡笔/油画棒颗粒、扁平高饱和色块、儿童画般的松动比例、极简五官、无真实光影的时装插画感。不复制参考图人物和服装。

【主体】保留用户照片中 2-5 个识别锚点：发型/帽子、脸型轮廓、眼神方向、身体姿态、服装主色、鞋子或配饰。人物五官大幅简化，眼睛是小黑点或短弧线，鼻子是一两笔细线，嘴巴是一小段色线。脸部不画真实骨骼结构，不画皮肤明暗，不画鼻梁高光，不画眼窝阴影。

【构图】1:1 方图，纯白或微暖白纸面背景。人物居中，保留原照片的大姿态和穿搭关系。背景完全干净，没有场景、没有环境光、没有投影。画面依靠人物色块和线条成立。

【风格】平面天真手绘时尚插画，蜡笔绘制，油画棒纹理，儿童比例，松散铅笔轮廓，简单色块，白色纸背景，编辑涂鸦肖像。

【色彩】使用鲜亮、直接的色块：天蓝、柠檬黄、橘色、草绿、暖粉肤色、白色。颜色像蜡笔直接涂在纸上，填色不均匀，有摩擦颗粒和轻微漏白。所有颜色是平涂，不做渐变、不做体积塑造。

【光影】完全取消真实光影系统。无高光、无阴影、无反光、无环境光、无摄影布光、无面部明暗过渡、无衣服褶皱阴影。只允许极少量手绘线条表示衣服边界和身体结构。

【材质】纸面感、蜡笔颗粒、彩铅摩擦纹理、松散线条、手绘抖动边缘。头发用密集短黑色笔触概括，不做发丝高光。衣服用大块平涂颜色概括，不画真实布料光泽。

【限制】避免写实感、照片感、真实皮肤、真实光影、厚重阴影、渐变色、3D 渲染、精致商业插画、日漫脸、复杂背景、摄影布光、皮肤磨皮、真实五官结构、衣服真实褶皱、AI 塑料感。不要保留原照片的明暗关系，只保留轮廓、姿态和识别锚点。
```

## 07 蜡笔特征头像

头肩构图，适合做头像。表情是第一优先级：嘴角、眼神、眉毛、微笑幅度都要轻微放大，变成标志性锚点。五官简化，但还得像本人。

```text
minimal crayon portrait, feature-exaggerated avatar

头肩构图，人物居中，适合作为社交媒体头像。
背景可用 pastel blue、cream blue、soft sky blue 或浅色纯色背景。

人物基于真实自拍，但不机械复制细节。先提取最有辨识度的 3 到 5 个特征：脸型、眉眼、鼻子、嘴部表情、发型轮廓、头部姿态与整体气质。表情最高优先级：抓住自拍里最有记忆点的表情信号，如嘴角方向、眼神力度、眉毛角度、微笑幅度或神态张力，并轻微放大，变成头像的标志性视觉锚点。

五官可简化但要像本人：眉毛更有性格，眼睛更直接，鼻子可作为暖色几何色块，嘴巴用简单色块线条强化表情，脸颊用 small soft blush，头发只保留大轮廓和关键发丝。可选一个辅助特征轻微夸张，但不能抢过表情。

笔触极简，线条少而松弛，大面积 flat color blocks，轻微 digital crayon edge。色彩明快单纯，可用 warm pink skin、coral red lips、orange nose accent、bright yellow clothing、soft white highlight。

重点表情放大、特征提炼与性格表达，弱化照片描摹。

限制：photorealism, complex brush, realistic skin, polished illustration, heavy crayon, 3D
```

## 08 旅行拼豆小人

截图里是海滩原图保留，右下角贴一个白边像素拼豆小人。小人要能一眼认出原照片：草帽、长发、蓝花发饰、蓝裙白袖都要对上。

截图只有效果图，没有完整原词。下面这版按画面反推，可直接用。

```text
Keep the uploaded travel photo as the full-bleed background. Do not crop, restyle, or illustrate the photograph itself. Preserve its original lighting, color, and composition.

On top of the photo, add one small perler-bead / fuse-bead pixel-art sticker of the same person.

Sticker rules:
- standing chibi full body, about 28%-38% of the image height
- placed in the lower-right, not covering the face
- thick white die-cut sticker border
- soft paper-like drop shadow so it reads as a physical sticker
- visible pixel grid, like ironed perler beads or cross-stitch
- cute kawaii proportions, large head, simple oval eyes, tiny blush
- match the source identity: hairstyle, hat, hair accessory, outfit colors, sleeve shape, and the most recognizable clothing details
- no background inside the sticker, only the character on transparent / white cutout
- no extra props unless they are already on the person

Style:
travel photo + one handmade pixel bead character sticker overlay.
Not anime, not 3D, not watercolor, not a full-image pixelation filter.

Avoid:
turning the whole photo into pixel art, covering the face, adding multiple stickers, logos, watermarks, text, frames, or a collage border.
```

只要单独的拼豆小人、不要原图时：

```text
Redraw the person in the uploaded photo as a standalone perler-bead pixel-art sticker on a plain white background.

Standing chibi full body, visible bead grid, thick white die-cut outline, cute simple face, signature outfit and accessories preserved. No scene, no photo background, no text.
```

## 09 极简手绘 IP

把人物或宠物收成一套固定手绘语言：粗黑线、平涂、小主体、大留白。适合头像、表情包、连续出系列。换场景时身份锁不能丢。

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: minimal hand-drawn character sticker illustration

Primary request: Redraw the character reference in one simple everyday scene.

Input images:
- Image 1: character reference; preserve identity, proportions, signature colors, and distinctive anatomy.

Character identity lock:
Keep species/class, dominant silhouette, head-to-body ratio, eye/mouth/ear/limb geometry, main and accent colors, distinctive accessories, and baseline mood.

Scene/action:
One small, quiet, readable action. One primary prop maximum. Deadpan or mildly awkward humor.

Scene/backdrop:
Pure or near-white empty background with broad margins. No environment or scenery.

Style/medium:
Minimal cute hand-drawn doodle and sticker illustration. Thick black outlines with slight natural wobble. Flat poster-color treatment: each enclosed region uses one uniform opaque fill with no internal tonal variation. Very few interior lines. Quiet deadpan expression.

Composition/framing:
One small centered character or compact character-and-prop cluster, broad empty margins, full silhouette visible, not filling the canvas. Subject about one-third to one-half of the canvas.

Constraints:
Keep the same character class, silhouette logic, feature placement, signature color blocks, distinctive anatomy, accessories, and baseline emotional energy. Keep props subordinate. No text unless requested.

Avoid:
Photorealism, realistic animal anatomy, 3D rendering, toy, clay, plush, fur, plastic, gradients, highlights, cast shadows, ambient shadows, uncontrolled grain, coarse texture, cinematic lighting, complex background, crowded objects, vector-logo cleanliness, watermark, and extra characters.
```

## 10 松弛童趣涂鸦

把人物、宠物或互动场面收成几分钟速写：线有点笨、色很少、背景删光。奇怪姿势不要纠正，那才是这张图最好笑的地方。

下面是 Red Skill `photo-to-doodle0826` 的原文，当普通提示词用。上传照片后整段复制即可。

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: standalone minimalist doodle illustration
Primary request: Redraw the supplied photo as a relaxed, childlike, slightly humorous hand-drawn doodle.
Input image: edit target and authoritative reference for subject, pose, expression, proportions, orientation, and main colors.

Turn the source photo into a standalone doodle illustration, not a redesigned character or a literal photo copy. It should feel as though an observant illustrator spent a few minutes capturing what is funniest and most recognizable about the source. Extremely simple, relaxed, childlike, slightly humorous. Pure white background. Not a photographic copy, polished mascot, standard cartoon, or before/after comparison.

Preserve from the source:
- exact action and pose category: standing, sitting, lying, crouching, curling, jumping, leaning, reaching, stretching, grooming, and so on
- head direction and tilt, torso lean, center of gravity, limb placement, and relationships between hands, feet, paws, wings, or tails
- facial direction, gaze, and the clearest emotion
- relative proportions and distinctive silhouette, including funny, awkward, accidental, or anatomically unusual positions
- interactions among multiple people or animals
- recognizable appearance: hairstyle, face shape impression, glasses, hats, facial hair, clothing silhouette and main colors, accessories, ear shape, tail, fur or feather markings, eye and mouth shapes, and unusual body features
- an object only when it is necessary to understand the action or joke; simplify it aggressively

Never redesign the pose, normalize a strange posture, beautify the subject, or invent a conflicting emotion. Mildly exaggerate an existing cue only when it strengthens recognition: rounder already-wide eyes, a clearer broad smile, a slightly stronger head tilt, or a more relaxed version of an already-lazy pose. When anatomical correctness conflicts with the funny or unusual source pose, preserve the source pose.

Simplification:
Reduce complex anatomy and surfaces to large, readable shapes. A slightly large head, long or short limbs, rounded body, narrow shoulders, oversized hands or feet, uneven outline, and asymmetry are welcome when they arise naturally from the source.
For people, use only a few marks for facial features. Do not render pores, wrinkles, realistic skin, detailed lighting, or fine garment texture.
For animals, keep the silhouette, ears, tail, limbs, markings, eyes, mouth, and special features. Do not draw individual hairs, feathers, scales, curls, or other realistic texture.
```
